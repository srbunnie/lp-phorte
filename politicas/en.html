1.3 Data Storage and Retention Periods

2.2 What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files downloaded to your device (computer, tablet, or smartphone) when visiting a website. They recognize interactions between you, our portals, and educational ads to optimize and personalize your browsing experience.

The term "cookie" also encompasses equivalent technologies such as tracking pixels, web beacons, and mobile device identifiers.

  • Session Cookies: Collect temporary technical data that does not directly identify you and are automatically deleted when you close the browser.
  • Persistent Cookies: Remain stored on your device until expiration or manual deletion, remembering your site preferences for future visits.
  • First-party vs. Third-party Cookies: First-party cookies are set directly by the Faculdade Phorte domain; third-party cookies belong to trusted external partners (e.g., Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, RD Station).

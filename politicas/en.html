1. Privacy Policy

How Associação de Educação e Novas Tecnologias Phorte processes personal data on Phorte websites, forms, the digital-course checkout, and services connected with course delivery.

✨ Key Takeaways: Fundamental Principles
  • We process data to answer requests, handle course orders and payments, issue receipts or tax documents, and provide access to digital courses.
  • Payment, technology, analytics, and communications providers may receive data needed for their services; browsing is not blanket consent for every use.
  • Non-essential cookies depend on your choice in the preference panel. You can also contact our Data Protection Officer to exercise privacy rights.

1.3 Data Storage and Retention Periods

There is no single retention period for all data. Each category is kept only as long as needed for its stated purpose and any applicable legal, tax, academic, or claims periods:

  • Application access logs: where required by Brazil’s Internet Civil Rights Framework, kept for the statutory minimum of six months under confidentiality and in a controlled environment;
  • Orders and tax documents: kept for the period required by tax, accounting, and consumer-protection duties;
  • Academic records: retained under the rules that apply to the educational service and academic archive;
  • Other data: kept until no longer needed for the purpose, unless another legal duty or the establishment, exercise, or defence of legal claims requires retention.

At the end of the applicable period, data is deleted or anonymized unless its retention is permitted or required by law. A deletion request does not override mandatory retention duties.

1.4 Legal Grounds and Consent

The legal basis is determined for each purpose and data set. To complete a requested purchase and provide a course, we may process data needed to perform the contract and steps requested by the buyer; billing and mandatory records may be processed to meet legal duties; optional analytics and advertising cookies depend on your cookie-panel choice. Checkout reminders and promotional communications are separate purposes and may have separate preference and unsubscribe controls. Legitimate interest is used only where permitted and after considering people’s rights and expectations.

Only data needed for the purposes described may be shared with:

  • Website/checkout and payment providers, including WooCommerce, Stripe, and PagBank, depending on the purchase flow;
  • Technical services that serve or protect pages, such as Google-hosted fonts, Cloudflare, and hCaptcha, where used;

Some providers may process data in other countries or allow access from abroad. Where this occurs, safeguards required by applicable law will be applied. Contact the Data Protection Officer for information about a particular provider or transfer.

1.6 Security Measures and Information Protection

We use technical and organizational safeguards proportionate to the data and risks. These may include access controls, protected data transfers, and internal procedures; safeguards vary by system and provider.

If a security incident presents a relevant risk or harm, we will take containment measures and notify the ANPD and affected people where and within the time limits required by applicable rules.

Cookies needed for requested functions, security, the cart, and recording that choice may operate separately, subject to applicable law.

2.2 What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small files stored in a browser or device. Pixels, local storage, and similar technologies may also record identifiers, pages visited, and events. Their nature and duration vary; session cookies are not necessarily removed by every browser when it closes.

The term "cookie" also encompasses equivalent technologies such as tracking pixels, web beacons, and mobile device identifiers.

  • Session cookies: remain for a session or for the period set by the service;
  • Persistent cookies: remain until the provider-defined expiration or manual deletion;
  • First-party and third-party cookies: may be set by the website or by external services loaded on a page.
